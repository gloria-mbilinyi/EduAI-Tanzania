## 2. PROBLEM STATEMENT

Many Tanzanian secondary schools experience:

1. Shortages of qualified subject teachers.
2. Limited personalized learning resources.
3. Difficulty monitoring academic progress.
4. Delayed school-parent communication.
5. Limited data-driven academic decisions.
6. Insufficient after-school learning assistance, particularly in rural communities.

Develop a centralized educational platform that integrates AI-assisted learning, academic management, teacher support, parent monitoring, administrative reporting, and subscription management.

Design it with Tanzanian educational realities in mind, including internet limitations, affordable devices, Kiswahili support, and relevant secondary-school subjects.

## 5. BUSINESS RULES

Enforce these rules in backend services, not only through frontend restrictions.

BR-01: Every student belongs to a registered school.

BR-02: A parent can view only explicitly linked students.

BR-03: Teachers can manage only assigned courses.

BR-04: AI recommendations must be approved by an authorized teacher before students can view them.

BR-05: Administrators can access authorized system and school reports according to their administrative scope.

BR-06: Every protected service requires authentication.

BR-07: Premium AI features are available only after verified payment and active subscription entitlement.

BR-08: Students cannot modify grades or teacher approvals.

BR-09: Authentication credentials must never be stored in plaintext.

BR-10: Sensitive student information must not be exposed to unrelated users.

BR-11: Payment verification must happen server-side, never through a client-submitted success flag.

BR-12: Maintain an audit trail of important actions.
